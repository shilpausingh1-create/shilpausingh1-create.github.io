Anthropic puts 5% of its shares in a public fund

Share

Anthropic, the maker of Claude, says it plans to put 5 percent of its shares into a new fund for the public good. The plan still needs approval from its shareholders. The company says none of the Anthropic shares held by the fund will be used for lobbying or political giving.

What happened

On October 18, 2026, Anthropic published a post about the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. It says the company is committing 5 percent of its shares to the fund. It also says the commitment needs stockholder approval.

The fund is set up as a wholly owned LLC. That is a type of company. Anthropic says it chose this over a charity format to keep more flexibility in how it gives. The fund could make large gifts to traditional nonprofits. It could also send money straight to groups of people. One example in the post is people hurt by AI's effects on jobs.

On politics, the post is clear. It says none of the Anthropic shares held by the fund will be used for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad.

Key facts

  • Starting share: 5 percent of Anthropic's shares, per Anthropic's post.
  • Approval: the plan needs stockholder approval.
  • Growth: Anthropic says its board may add 2 percent for every 50 percent rise in the company's value above a set starting point. The post does not say what that starting point is.
  • Combined total: with earlier commitments, about 20 percent of Anthropic's shares will go toward the public good, plus any added later, per the post.
  • Earlier pledges: the post says the co-founders pledged 80 percent of their own equity, and about half of employees use a donation match program.
  • Jobs research: Anthropic says it has separately committed $200 million to study how AI affects the job market.

Background

Anthropic says it wants the share given to the public good to grow as the company grows. The post says that instead of bigger and bigger pay awards for leaders, it is directing that equity toward public benefit.

Bloomberg also reported on the plan. The details here come from Anthropic's own post.

What it means for you

You will not see anything change in Claude today. This is about how a very valuable company plans to share some of that value.

Our take: this is a promise from the company, not a finished deal. Until shareholders approve it, and until details on how the fund is run are shared, it is hard to judge. The "no lobbying or political giving" line is a clear, checkable rule, so it is worth holding Anthropic to.

The post does not say how much money the shares are worth. Share value depends on the company's price, which can move.

What happens next

Anthropic says it has not yet shared how the fund will be structured or governed. It says it will share more in the coming months, including the value milestones that could add to the fund's stake. It also plans to publish a public benefit report on its progress.

FAQ

How much of Anthropic is going into the fund?

Anthropic says 5 percent of its shares, if stockholders approve, per its own post.

Can the fund pay for lobbying?

No. The post says none of the Anthropic equity held by the LLC will be used for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad.

What will the fund pay for?

The post gives examples: large gifts to nonprofits and direct help to groups, such as people affected by AI's impact on jobs. It says more details will come in the next few months.
