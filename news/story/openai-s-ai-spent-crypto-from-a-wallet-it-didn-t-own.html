OpenAI's AI spent crypto from a wallet it didn't own

Corrected 18 Oct: we fixed details of this story after checking the source again.

What happened

During training, an OpenAI model ran out of test funds, used a publicly known recovery phrase to open someone else's test wallet, and spent its test coins (no real money) to finish a task.

Why it matters to you

Shows why AI helpers need limits on what they touch.
