DeepSeek raises about $15 billion in funding round

By the ITG Now newsroom · News · 22 Oct 2026, 07:47 IST

Share

Chinese AI company DeepSeek is reported to be raising a very large funding round. Early reports put it at 80 billion yuan or more, with a possible top end of 100 billion yuan. CATL and Tencent are said to be among the largest backers.

What happened

On October 6, 2026, Bloomberg News reported that DeepSeek was close to securing at least 80 billion yuan, about $11.93 billion. CATL and Tencent Holdings were said to have committed among the largest amounts.

The round began at about 50 billion yuan. Demand grew after DeepSeek launched its latest AI model, and Bloomberg said the final total could approach 100 billion yuan. At the rate implied by the 80 billion yuan = $11.93 billion figure, 100 billion yuan is about $15 billion (our math). That is only a possible top end, not an amount raised.

The numbers come from a Bloomberg News report. DeepSeek has not announced the round, per TNW, and Reuters could not immediately verify the report, per Khaleej Times.

Key facts

  • At least 80 billion yuan (about $11.93 billion) was reported as near, per Bloomberg on October 6, 2026.
  • The first target was about 50 billion yuan. Bloomberg said the total could approach 100 billion yuan.
  • CATL and Tencent are said to be among the largest backers.
  • DeepSeek, Tencent and CATL did not immediately respond to requests for comment, per Khaleej Times.
  • The round's valuation has not been reported in these articles.

Background

This would follow DeepSeek's earlier round. That round raised about 50 billion yuan at a value of $52 billion to $59 billion, TNW reports. Tencent put in roughly 10 billion yuan and CATL about 5 billion yuan. The company's founder put in about 20 billion yuan.

DeepSeek grew out of a quant hedge fund called High-Flyer. It drew wide attention in early 2025 with its open-source R1 model and launched its V4 models this year, per TNW.

Reuters also reported that DeepSeek hired CITIC Securities to prepare a possible listing on Shanghai's STAR Market, per Khaleej Times. Timing and size are not set.

What it means for you

This matters most to people who follow AI competition and to anyone who uses or builds on DeepSeek's open models. The round is not confirmed, and DeepSeek has not announced it. If you use DeepSeek's models in your work, nothing changes today, so keep using them as before. Watch for an official announcement, the final round size, and any listing plans, because those would show how much money the company really has. Our take: a round this size would show investors still back China's AI labs, but the final size is unproven until DeepSeek says so.
