Best AI models by use case, explained simply

Updated 2026-10-17 18:00 IST · By the Git Trending Global team · Every number links its source

Quick answer

There is no single best AI model. On the tests below, the leaders are: coding: Claude Opus 5.5 on LMArena WebDev; reasoning and math: Gemini 4 Argon on LiveBench 2.0 Reasoning; chat and writing: Gemini 4 Argon on LMArena Text; speed and price: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on Output speed. Pick the use case you care about and tap a model to see its price, size and every score with its source.

How to read this

  • What a benchmark is: a fixed test, like an exam, that every model takes so you can compare them on the same questions.
  • Maker's own numbers can be biased: a company picks the tests it does well on. Scores tagged "independent" were run by an outside group.
  • Ranks change fast: new models come out every few weeks, so check the "as of" date on each chart.
  • One number never tells the whole story: price, speed, privacy and how a model feels to use matter too. Try two or three on your own tasks.
