How to read this
  • What a benchmark is: a fixed test, like an exam, that every model takes so you can compare them on the same questions.
  • Maker's own numbers can be biased: a company picks the tests it does well on. Scores tagged "independent" were run by an outside group.
  • Ranks change fast: new models come out every few weeks, so check the "as of" date on each test.
  • One number never tells the whole story: price, speed, privacy and how a model feels to use matter too. Try two or three on your own tasks.
What we left out on purpose
  • Vision: no trusted vision score covers these models yet, so vision is left out.
  • Left out on purpose: SWE-bench and Aider (none of these models on the official boards yet), a maker's number for a rival's model, and averages we would have to compute ourselves.
