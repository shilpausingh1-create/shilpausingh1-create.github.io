Vorssaint Utils: one free Mac app instead of a dozen paid ones

Vorssaint Utils is a free Mac menu bar app with per-app volume, window snapping, clipboard history, a system monitor and much more.

EveryoneCreatorsBusiness

stars: 27K · license: GPL-3.0 · platforms: Mac

The problem it solves

Macs are great, but they miss some basic things. You cannot set the volume for each app. Snapping windows side by side is clunky. There is no proper clipboard history.

So many Mac users buy small apps to fill the gaps. One for volume. One for windows. One for the clipboard. One to uninstall apps cleanly. Each costs a few dollars or a subscription, and each adds another icon to your menu bar.

Vorssaint Utils puts all of that behind one icon, for free.

What it is

Vorssaint Utils is a free, open-source Mac app that lives in your menu bar (the strip of icons at the top of your screen). Open source means anyone can read its code.

The README calls it "one menu bar icon doing the job of a dozen paid Mac apps." It promises no account, no telemetry (usage tracking sent back to the maker) and no subscription.

The best part: you only install what you use. Pick single features or start with a preset. Features you skip do not load and do not clutter the screen. Setup only asks for the permissions your chosen features need.

Who it's for

  • Anyone tired of paying for lots of tiny Mac apps.
  • Remote workers who want music on speakers while a call uses their headset.
  • Video makers and streamers who need screen recording and quick edits.
  • Writers and assistants who reuse the same text and want snippets and clipboard history.
  • People with older mice or keyboards that double-click or repeat letters.

What you can do with it

The feature list is long. Here are highlights from the README:

Sound - Set volume per app, and boost quiet audio past 100%. - Send each app to a different speaker or headset. - Mute all microphones with one shortcut.

Know what your Mac is doing - A system monitor for CPU, graphics, memory, temperatures and battery health. - Fan control (in beta) with manual speeds or temperature curves. - Alerts for high heat, low disk space or low battery.

Windows and the Dock - Snap windows into layouts and move them between screens. - Preview windows by hovering over Dock icons. - Protection against accidental Command-Q or Command-W.

Keyboard and mouse - Text snippets that expand short codes into full text. - Smooth scrolling and separate scroll direction for mouse and trackpad. - Filters that ignore accidental double-clicks from a worn mouse or repeated keys from a worn keyboard.

Clipboard, files and links - Searchable clipboard history for text, images and files. - Paste as plain text. - A "shelf" to park files near your cursor while dragging.

Everyday tools - A Dynamic Island around the camera notch for music, timers and more, or a simulated one on other Macs. - A command bar to search apps, files and menus, do math and convert units. - An autosaving scratchpad for notes.

Capture and create - Screenshots with crop, notes, blur and scrolling capture. - Screen recording with trimming, automatic zooms and GIF export. - Copy text from anywhere on screen, offline.

App management - An uninstaller that also finds leftover files. - A cleaner for caches and logs.

Energy and display - Keep your Mac awake on a timer or with the lid closed. - Brightness control for external displays.

How to start, step by step

What you need first: a Mac with Apple Silicon (an M-series chip) running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

  1. Go to the Releases page on the project's GitHub.
  2. Download the disk image (the .dmg file).
  3. Open it and drag Vorssaint into your Applications folder.
  4. Open Vorssaint.
  5. Choose the features you want, or start with a preset.
  6. Grant only the permissions it asks for. It explains each one in plain words.

If you use Homebrew (a free installer for Mac apps), you can install it with one line instead:

brew install --cask vorssaint

The builds are signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens them without scary warnings.

Good to know

  • Apple Silicon only. Older Intel Macs are not listed as supported.
  • It is free and will stay free, according to the README. You can tip the maker through Buy Me a Coffee if you like it.
  • Private by default. No account, analytics or tracking. The README says it only goes online for things you can see, like update checks, the speed test, optional song lyrics, and temporary share links you create.
  • Permissions are optional. The app shows which features use each permission, and even tells you when one is no longer needed.
  • Fan control is in beta, so treat it with care.
  • License, in plain words. It uses GPL 3.0 or later. You can use, study, change and share it. If you share a changed version, you must share its code under the same license. The Vorssaint name and logo are protected separately.

The bottom line

Vorssaint Utils replaces a whole folder of small paid Mac apps with one free menu bar icon. You only turn on what you need, and it keeps your data on your Mac. If you have an Apple Silicon Mac, it is an easy win.

Get it: github.com/vorssaint/vorssaint-utils
