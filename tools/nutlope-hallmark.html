Hallmark: stop your AI from building websites that look AI-made

Hallmark is a free design add-on for AI coding helpers that steers them away from generic, cookie-cutter web pages.

DesignMarketingCreators

stars: 30K · license: MIT · platforms: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex

The problem it solves

Ask an AI to build you a website and you will get something that works. But it often looks like every other AI-built site. Same big headline. Same purple glow. Same three boxes in a row.

People notice. A site that looks "made by AI" can feel cheap, even if the business behind it is great. For a bakery, a band, a shop, or a new app, that first look matters.

Hallmark was made to fix this. It teaches your AI helper better design taste, so two sites for two different ideas actually feel different.

What it is

Hallmark is a free design "skill" for AI coding helpers. A skill is a set of instructions an AI follows for a certain kind of job. Hallmark's job is web design. It works with Claude Code, Cursor and Codex.

It was made by Together AI. The README describes it as a design skill that "refuses to look AI-generated."

Here is how it works, in plain words. When you describe your site, Hallmark first picks an overall page shape that fits your idea. Then it dresses that shape in one of 21 themes. Before handing the page back, it runs 57 checks for the tired, generic patterns AI tools fall into by default. It also does a final self-review.

When your idea does not fit any of the 21 themes, Hallmark switches to a Custom mode. It designs the page from scratch, with its own colors, fonts and layout. The same 57 checks still apply.

Who it's for

  • Small business owners who want a site that feels like their brand, not a template.
  • Freelance designers who use AI to speed up first drafts.
  • Marketers building landing pages for a launch or campaign.
  • Musicians, artists and makers who want a site with personality.
  • Founders shipping a product page fast.
  • Developers who build websites with AI helpers every day.

You do need to use one of the supported AI coding helpers. If you already "vibe code" (build apps by chatting with AI), this fits right in.

What you can do with it

Straight from the README, Hallmark has four main actions:

  • Build new pages. This is the default. Describe your site and it picks a layout, applies its design rules, and runs its checks.
  • Audit an existing site. Ask it to score your current code against its list of bad patterns. You get a to-do list, with no edits made.
  • Redesign. It throws out the old structure but keeps your words, page order and brand, then rebuilds with a fresh look.
  • Study a design you admire. Give it a screenshot or a web address. It pulls out the "DNA" of that design: the layout, font pairing and main color. It refuses to make pixel-perfect copies or copy paid templates.

Other things worth knowing:

  • 21 themes, picked to match your brief.
  • 57 checks against generic AI design, plus a self-critique before it finishes.
  • Custom mode for ideas no theme fits.
  • A handoff file. The study action can write a simple design notes file you can give to other AI tools.

The README shows real examples: a sourdough app, a record label, a honey farm, a tea menu, a print fair, a travel booking site and a fashion brand. Each one looks clearly different. You can browse them on the live demo site, usehallmark.com.

How to start, step by step

What you need first: a computer with Node.js installed (a free program that runs many developer tools), and one of the supported AI helpers: Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex.

  1. Open Terminal (on a Mac) or your command window.
  2. Paste this and press Enter:
npx skills add nutlope/hallmark
  1. Open your AI helper as you normally would.
  2. Ask for a website. Describe your brand, who it is for, and the feeling you want. For example: "Build a homepage for a small family-run honey farm. Warm, earthy, a bit old-fashioned."
  3. Look at the result. If you want changes, just ask.

To update Hallmark later, run the same install command again.

Good to know

  • Hallmark is free. It uses the MIT license. In plain words, you can use it, change it, and use it in paid work. You just keep the license note.
  • The AI helper may cost money. Claude Code, Cursor and Codex usually need their own account or paid plan. Hallmark does not change that.
  • It guides, it does not guarantee. Hallmark gives the AI rules and checks. The final quality still depends on the AI model and on how clearly you describe what you want.
  • It respects other people's work. The study action is built to learn the feel of a design, not to clone it.

The bottom line

If your AI-built sites all look alike, Hallmark is an easy upgrade. One command adds real design rules and 57 quality checks to the AI helper you already use. Your next site has a better shot at looking like you, not like a template.
