Awesome DESIGN.md: free style guides that make AI build nicer pages

A free collection of ready-made design guides, based on popular websites, that AI tools read to build pages with a consistent, polished look.

DesignDevelopersMarketing

stars: 121K · license: MIT · platforms: Works with any AI coding tool

The problem it solves

AI tools can now build a web page in minutes. But have you noticed? Many of those pages look the same. Same fonts. Same purple gradient. Same plain buttons. And if you ask for a second page, it may not even match the first.

That is because the AI has no clear idea of how your page should look. It just guesses.

Awesome DESIGN.md gives the AI a clear style guide to follow, so your pages look polished and stay consistent.

What it is

Awesome DESIGN.md is a free collection of ready-to-use design files. Each one describes the look and feel of a well-known website, like Stripe, Notion, Airbnb, Apple or Spotify.

The files use a format called DESIGN.md. Google Stitch introduced the idea. It is a plain text file that describes how a project should look: its colors, fonts, buttons, spacing and more. AI tools read it and follow it.

There is nothing to install. No design software, no special tools. You copy one text file into your project and tell your AI to use it. At the time of the README, the collection had 84 design files.

Who it's for

  • Founders and small businesses building a website or app with AI.
  • Marketers who need landing pages that look professional, fast.
  • Designers who want to hand AI tools a clear visual direction.
  • "Vibe coders" (people who build apps by chatting with AI) who want better-looking results.
  • Developers who want consistent design without hiring a designer for a prototype.

What you can do with it

From the README:

  • Pick from many styles. The collection spans AI companies, developer tools, finance apps, online stores, media brands and even car makers. Examples include Claude, Cursor, Stripe, Notion, Figma, Airbnb, Nike, Spotify, Tesla and Ferrari.
  • Go retro. A "Retro Web" series captures 1990s and early 2000s site styles, like Dell in 1996.
  • Get a full style guide in each file. Every DESIGN.md covers:
  • the overall mood and feel
  • the color palette and what each color is for
  • font choices and sizes
  • how buttons, cards, menus and form fields look
  • spacing and layout rules
  • shadows and depth
  • do's and don'ts
  • how the design adapts to phones and tablets
  • ready-made prompts to give your AI
  • Preview before you pick. Each site comes with preview pages showing its colors, text styles, buttons and cards, in both light and dark versions.
  • Use any AI tool. The README says any AI coding agent or Google Stitch can read it.
  • Ask for a new one. You can request a DESIGN.md for a specific website through the getdesign.md site.

How to start, step by step

What you need first: an AI coding tool or web builder you already use, like Claude Code, Cursor or Google Stitch, and a project folder.

  1. Browse the collection on the GitHub page or at getdesign.md, and pick a style you like.
  2. Open that site's DESIGN.md file and copy it.
  3. Save it as DESIGN.md in the main (top) folder of your project.
  4. Tell your AI agent to use it. For example: "Build me a landing page that follows DESIGN.md."
  5. Look at the result, then keep asking for new pages. They should all match the same style.

Good to know

  • These describe other brands' looks. The README says the files capture publicly visible style values from real websites, and that the project does not claim ownership of any site's visual identity. Treat them as a starting point, and make sure your final design is your own, especially for a real business.
  • Results depend on your AI tool. The file guides the AI, but the quality of the page still depends on the tool you use.
  • Cost: the collection is free. Your AI tool may have its own costs.
  • No warranty: files are shared "as is," so colors or details may sometimes be off. The project welcomes fixes.
  • License (MIT): free to use, change and even use in a business. Just keep the license notice.

The bottom line

Awesome DESIGN.md is the fastest way to stop your AI-built pages from looking generic. Copy one text file, point your AI at it, and get a consistent, polished look. It is free, simple, and works with almost any AI tool.
