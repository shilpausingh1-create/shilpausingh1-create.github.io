Thaw: clean up your crowded Mac menu bar for free

Thaw is a free, open-source Mac app that hides, sorts and styles the cluttered icons in your menu bar, with no tracking and no account.

Everyone

stars: 12K · license: GPL-3.0 · platforms: Mac

The problem it solves

Look at the top right of your Mac screen. That strip is the menu bar. Every app you install seems to add a tiny icon there. Wi-Fi, battery, cloud storage, chat apps, password managers, and more.

Soon it is crowded. On a MacBook with a notch (the black cutout at the top of the screen), some icons can disappear behind it. You can't find what you need, and the clutter is distracting.

What it is

Thaw is a free, open-source menu bar manager for the Mac. Open source means anyone can read the code that runs it.

It lets you hide the icons you don't need, keep the ones you do, and find any icon fast with a keyboard shortcut. You can even change how the menu bar looks.

The makers are clear about it: free, and free to keep. No purchase, no subscription, and no upgrade fee when Apple ships a new macOS.

Who it's for

  • Any Mac user with too many icons up top.
  • MacBook owners whose icons get hidden behind the notch.
  • People who present or share their screen and want a clean look.
  • Anyone who likes a tidy, calm desktop.
  • People who like to customize how their Mac looks.

What you can do with it

  • Hide icons you rarely use. There is also an "always hidden" section for icons you almost never need.
  • Show them when you want. Reveal hidden icons by hovering, clicking, scrolling, swiping, or pressing a shortcut.
  • Search for any icon. Find and open a menu bar item from the keyboard.
  • Drag to rearrange. Put icons in the order you like, and group related ones together.
  • Use a second bar. Show hidden icons in a separate bar, handy for MacBooks with a notch.
  • Set smart rules. Show an icon only when something happens, like low battery. More rules, like Wi-Fi network or Focus mode, can be turned on in settings. Some are still marked experimental.
  • Switch setups. Save different layouts as profiles and link them to a screen, a desktop space, or a Focus mode.
  • Zen mode. One action hides everything. It can turn on by itself while you present or share your screen.
  • Style the bar. Add a tint, gradient, shadow, border or custom shape. It can even pick colors from your wallpaper.
  • Simple Mode. One easy settings page if you don't want to see every option.
  • Use your language. Thaw comes in 20 languages.
  • Connect other tools. Works with Raycast, Shortcuts, Siri and more.

How to start, step by step

What you need first: a Mac. Which version of Thaw you get depends on your macOS.

Your Mac runs What to do You get
macOS 27 brew install thaw@beta Thaw 3 (beta)
macOS 26 brew install thaw Thaw 2 (stable)
macOS 14 or 15 Download from Releases Thaw 1

The commands above use Homebrew, a free app installer you run in Terminal. No Homebrew? That is fine:

  1. Go to the Thaw Releases page on GitHub.
  2. Download the disk image (a .dmg file) for your macOS version.
  3. Open it and drag Thaw into your Applications folder.
  4. Open Thaw.
  5. When asked, allow Accessibility access. Thaw needs this to move menu bar icons. On macOS 27 it also asks for Menu Bar Layout Access.
  6. Choose which icons to hide.

You can also try Thaw in your web browser on getthaw.app before you install it.

Good to know

  • It is free. No purchase, no subscription.
  • No tracking. The README says there are no analytics, no telemetry and no sign-up.
  • Screen Recording is optional. Hiding and moving icons work without it. Grant it only if you want live icon previews and wallpaper-based colors.
  • Mac only. There is no Windows or Linux version.
  • Trusted builds. The app is signed and checked by Apple.
  • License: GPL-3.0. You are free to use it and change it. If you share a changed version, you must share its code too.

The bottom line

Thaw tidies your Mac menu bar so you see only what matters. It is free, private, and packed with options, from simple hiding to smart rules and custom colors. If your menu bar feels crowded, it is an easy fix.

Get it: github.com/thaw-app/Thaw
