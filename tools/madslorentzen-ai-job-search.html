AI Job Search: the free kit that tailors your CV for every job

An AI job-hunting kit that scores job posts, tailors your CV and cover letter, and builds interview prep packs.

Jobs & CareersEveryone

stars: 47K · license: MIT · platforms: Runs on your computer with Claude Code (paid Claude plan needed)

The problem it solves

Job hunting is a second full-time job. Every posting wants a slightly different CV. Every application wants a fresh cover letter. Then you need to research the company and prepare for interviews. Do that 50 times and your evenings and weekends vanish.

Sending the same CV everywhere is faster, but it rarely works. Tailored applications get noticed. They just take forever to write.

What it is

AI Job Search is a free, open-source job application kit that runs on your own computer. It is built on Claude Code (Anthropic's AI helper that works with files on your computer). You fill in your profile once. Then the AI scores job postings, tailors your CV, writes cover letters, and helps you prepare for interviews.

The creator is a geophysicist. When his job was cut in late 2025, he built this kit for his own search. He says it led to 69 tailored applications, 20 first interviews and one signed contract. He started as an AI engineer in June 2026.

The project says it is independent and is not affiliated with Anthropic.

Who it's for

  • Anyone job hunting who is tired of rewriting the same CV.
  • People changing careers, since it can spot skills that carry over to new fields.
  • Recent graduates sending lots of applications at once.
  • Laid-off professionals who need to move fast and stay organized.
  • Career coaches who want a structured way to help clients.

The core workflow works in any language and any country. The built-in job search tools are made for Denmark, plus LinkedIn and one tech job site that work anywhere.

What you can do with it

  • Build your profile. Feed it your CV, LinkedIn export, diplomas and reference letters, or just answer interview-style questions.
  • Search job sites. Find jobs that match your profile, with duplicates removed and results sorted by fit.
  • Rank a big batch. Score many jobs at once and get a shortlist with honest strengths and gaps for each.
  • Score each job's fit across skills, experience, culture, location and career goals.
  • Draft a tailored CV and cover letter for each job, as polished PDFs.
  • Get a second opinion. A separate AI "reviewer" researches the company, critiques the drafts, and the drafter revises.
  • Check robot readability. It tests that hiring software (called an ATS, the system companies use to filter CVs) can read your CV properly.
  • Never fake it. The README says every claim is checked against your real profile, and it never makes up skills.
  • Prep for interviews. It builds a prep pack for each interview, maps likely questions to your real examples, and offers a mock interview.
  • Track your search. Record outcomes, see follow-ups, and make a dashboard of your applications.
  • Find skill gaps. Get a learning plan based on the jobs you want.
  • Add your local job site if you are outside Denmark.

How to start, step by step

First you need:

  • Claude Code. It has no free tier. You need a paid Claude plan or Anthropic API credits (pay as you go).
  • Python 3.10 or newer.
  • Bun (a free tool that runs the job search add-ons).
  • LaTeX (free software that turns text into neat PDFs), such as MacTeX on a Mac or MiKTeX on Windows.

Then:

  1. Make your own copy. The quick way is this command:
gh repo fork MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search --clone
cd ai-job-search

Important: a "fork" on GitHub is always public, and your personal details get saved in it. For your own search, the README recommends a private copy instead. Follow SETUP.md section 8 for the two-minute steps.

  1. Install the job search tools:
python3 tools/install_portal_clis.py
  1. Start Claude Code by typing claude, then type /setup. Share your CV or answer its questions.
  2. Type /scrape to search for jobs.
  3. Type /apply followed by a job link, or paste the job description, to get a tailored CV and cover letter.
  4. When you land an interview, use /interview to prepare.

Good to know

  • It costs money to run. The kit is free, but Claude Code needs a paid plan or API credits.
  • Keep your copy private. Your name, contact details, work history and salary hopes are stored in its files.
  • Job sites are Denmark-first. Outside Denmark, start with the LinkedIn search or add your own job board.
  • Read before you send. The README says job postings are treated as untrusted, but advises you to skim what was fetched and written before hitting send.
  • Watch for scams. The project says it has no crypto token or paid sponsorship program. The only ways to support it are Ko-fi and GitHub contributions.
  • Other AI tools. There are notes for Codex, Gemini CLI and others, but it is built for Claude Code.
  • License. It uses MIT. In plain words: free to use, change and share, as long as you keep the copyright notice.

The bottom line

AI Job Search turns the slow parts of job hunting into a guided workflow: fit scores, tailored CVs, cover letters and interview prep. It helped its own creator get hired. If you already pay for Claude, it can save you many evenings of rewriting.
