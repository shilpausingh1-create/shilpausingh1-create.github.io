Brag: turn your finished project into a launch video, free

A free AI add-on that turns the project you built into a short launch video with music, motion and ready-to-post text.

CreatorsMarketingDevelopers

stars: 16K · license: MIT · platforms: Claude Code, Codex and other AI agents

The problem it solves

You finally finished your project. Maybe it's a website, an app or a small tool. Now you want to tell the world.

But a good launch video takes time. You need to plan the story, pick music, make things move on screen, and write the post that goes with it. Most people don't have the time or the video skills. So the project gets shared with a plain link and a screenshot, and few people notice.

Brag makes the video for you with one command.

What it is

Brag is a free, open-source "skill" for AI helpers. A skill is an add-on that teaches an AI helper a new job.

Once it's installed, you open your project and type /brag. The AI looks at what you built, plans the story, and makes a short, shareable launch video. It includes music, motion and share copy (the text you post with the video).

The original version uses a video tool called Hyperframes to build and render the video. There's also a newer, lighter version called /brag-slim made for Claude Opus 5.5, which doesn't need Hyperframes.

Fun fact: the looping video on Brag's own website was made by Brag.

Who it's for

  • Indie makers and solo founders who build things and need to show them off fast.
  • Developers who launch side projects and hate making videos.
  • Small teams and marketers who want a quick first draft of a launch clip.
  • Creators who build tools or sites and share them online.

You need to be using an AI coding helper such as Claude Code or Codex. If you don't, see the "Good to know" section for a no-setup option.

What you can do with it

  • Make a launch video with one command: type /brag (or "let's /brag") in your project.
  • Get a video built around your project, not a generic template.
  • Get music and share copy included.
  • Set the tone, for example: /brag --tone "fake Series A launch from 2016".
  • Add a voiceover if you want one with /brag --voice. It's off by default.
  • Get all the files. You get a brag-output/ folder with the plan, a brief, the share copy and the finished brag.mp4 video.
  • Use it in many AI helpers, including Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini CLI, opencode and Google Antigravity.

How to start, step by step

You need an AI coding helper (like Claude Code or Codex). For the full version, the README also lists Node.js 22 or newer, FFmpeg (a free video tool) and the Hyperframes command-line tool.

In Claude Code:

  1. Add Brag's marketplace (a list of add-ons you can install). Type:
/plugin marketplace add latent-spaces/brag
  1. Install it:
/plugin install brag@brag
  1. Open your project and type /brag.

In Codex:

codex plugin marketplace add latent-spaces/brag
codex plugin add brag@brag

In any other AI helper, run this one command in Terminal:

npx skills add https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag --skill brag

Then ask your helper: "let's /brag". When it's done, open the brag-output/ folder to find your video.

Good to know

  • No-setup option: the Brag team also runs letsbrag.app. You paste your site's link and get a 20-second launch video. The README says no setup and no subscription. The open-source skill stays free.
  • Your AI helper does the work. Brag runs inside your AI helper, so it uses that helper's normal plan or usage. Check what your AI plan costs.
  • Windows users may need a small Git setting for the add-on to be found. The README explains the fix, or you can copy the skill folder by hand.
  • Music credits: the bundled music comes from ende.app and sound effects from Kenney, as listed in the README.
  • MIT license means you can use, change and share it for free, even for business. Just keep the copyright notice.

The bottom line

Brag turns "I built a thing" into a ready-to-post launch video in one step. It's free, works in many AI helpers, and saves hours of video work. If you build things and want people to notice, it's worth a try.

Get it: github.com/latent-spaces/brag
