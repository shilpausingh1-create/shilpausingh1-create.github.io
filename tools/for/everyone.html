Why these picks

These are free tools for everyday life on your computer, no coding job required. You'll find private AI assistants that chat with you in WhatsApp or Telegram, offline voice typing, a workout tracker with no subscription, free Mac cleaners, a menu bar tidy-up app, and a way to turn any website into its own desktop app. A few help with privacy: they run AI on your own computer, so your chats and notes stay on it. There are job search helpers too, which check job posts against your CV. Every guide is written in plain words, with copy-paste setup steps and our honest take on each tool.
