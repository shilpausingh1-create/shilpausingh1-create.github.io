These free tools help you make campaigns, pages and content faster. Are these AI tools for marketers free?

All 9 tools here are open source on GitHub. By license: MIT (6), AGPL-3.0 (2), Apache-2.0 (1). A tool can be free while an AI service it connects to is not, so check the price row in each guide's at-a-glance table.
