Why these picks

These free tools help you run a small business or a team with less busywork. You'll find private AI assistants that live in WhatsApp, Slack or Telegram, a meeting note taker that keeps recordings on your own computer, AI that writes real Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and an SEO tool that works as a free Semrush alternative. A few help you keep AI bills in check or manage several AI agents from one dashboard. Many of them run on your own computer or server, so your data stays with you. Each guide gives the setup steps, real examples you can try today, and our honest take on what the tool is good and bad at.
