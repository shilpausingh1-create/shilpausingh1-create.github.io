Why these picks

These free tools are for people who make videos, podcasts, photos and posts. There is a browser video editor with no watermark, a free CapCut alternative, a screen recorder that adds auto-zooms, AI green screen removal that keeps fine hair, and an AI video maker that scripts and edits a video from a plain description. For audio, there is offline voice typing and a transcription tool that labels who spoke and when. For photos, there are two Photoshop-style editors that open PSD files. Every guide shows how to install the tool step by step and what it costs, with honest pros and cons so you can pick fast.

Each guide below is for a tool whose own README, examples and "who it's for" notes fit creators. Every guide has setup steps from the tool's own page.
