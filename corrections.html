Corrections

When we get a fact wrong, we fix the story, add a dated note that says what changed, and list it here. Spotted a mistake? Send us a message on Instagram.

  • Ex-OpenAI engineer in bugging case held as flight risk (20 Oct story)

    Corrected 20 Oct: no opened source says prosecutors claim Saudi backing. Filings cite a one-way trip to Saudi Arabia as flight risk; Saudi ties come from his podcast comments and a venture role at a Riyadh firm. Headline and source fixed.

  • Reuters: hacker used AI agents against 100+ company sites (20 Oct story)

    Corrected 20 Oct: Deere and Cummins were not confirmed breached (Cummins is investigating a vendor's site; Deere saw no sign sensitive data was accessed). Claude Code use is 'possible', per NorForge. Researcher named by role only.

  • Microsoft pulls out of a UK AI data center deal (19 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Microsoft gave no reason. 'No grid power until the 2030s' is the Telegraph's report.

  • DeepSeek says free model update tops a coding test (18 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: The sources never name Claude; 'chases Claude' was our inference from the Hugging Face page, which lists Opus 5 at 74.0 vs DeepSeek's 77.0 on DeepSWE (DeepSeek's own table). Headline changed 19 Oct to a neutral, sourced one (77.0 on DeepSWE in DeepSeek's own table, vs Opus 5 74.0).

  • DeepSeek opens search API at $0.75 per 1,000 (18 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Search and Fetch both cost $0.75 per 1,000 successful calls. Only server-side calls via DeepSeek Harness, Claude Code or Codex are free until 1 Nov 00:00 Beijing time.

  • Anthropic signs first Canada data center deal (18 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: The 20-year lease, 95 MW and late-2027 start are EverWind's claims; EverWind does not name the tenant. Reuters names Anthropic.

  • Anthropic quietly pitches its own AI safety bill (18 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Threshold: a foreseeable, imminent risk of more than 50 people killed or seriously injured, or over $1B in damage.

  • $137M of AI-made ads flood the US midterms (18 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: $137M counts ads made at least partly with AI; 27% carried an AI label.

  • Microsoft finishes training its giant MAI-Base-2 (18 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Pre-training is finished; post-training is underway. '4 to 12x' is pre-training compute efficiency versus DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base only, a scaling-law estimate (4.2 to 12.3x), per Microsoft.

  • Disney hires Character.AI's team after bankruptcy (18 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Disney pays $20M for a license to Character.AI's code and 9 patents, not the platform. About $180M is Disney's 3-year cost estimate for the staff it hires. Character.AI filed Chapter 11 on 12 Oct in Delaware.

  • Apple spells out its ban on AI nudify apps (17 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Not a new ban: Apple says these apps were already banned. Rule 2.5.19 spells it out and adds penalties.

  • iPhone Duo sells out, deliveries slip to 2027 (17 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: 'Three months' is in no source. Delivery dates vary by outlet (25 or 29 January 2027).

  • Claude solves a 25-year-old encryption mystery (17 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Anthropic says a backdoor is 'much less plausible', not ruled out. B-163 and some base points are still unexplained.

  • Microsoft builds a giant 2.8 trillion parameter AI (16 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Pre-training is finished; post-training is underway. '4 to 12x' is pre-training compute efficiency versus DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base only, a scaling-law estimate (4.2 to 12.3x), per Microsoft.

  • Meta gives away its big Muse Spark AI for free (16 Oct story)

    Corrected 19 Oct: Muse Spark runs on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios (up to 512 GB each).

  • OpenAI's AI spent crypto from a wallet it didn't own (16 Oct story)

    Corrected 18 Oct: earlier text said 'in testing' and 'found a public wallet key'. CoinDesk: it happened in TRAINING of an internal GPT-5.6 Sol-family model (26 Jun, found 25 Sep), 362 addresses from public recovery phrases, Sepolia testnet only.
