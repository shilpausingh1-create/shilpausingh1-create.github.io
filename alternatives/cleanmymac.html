FAQ

What is a free alternative to CleanMyMac?

PureMac is a free CleanMyMac alternative. It clears junk, fully uninstalls apps and finds duplicates, with no subscription, no tracking and no scare tactics. Mole is a free Mac cleaner you run in Terminal. It clears junk, fully removes apps, maps your disk and shows your Mac's health. Open source, no subscription.

Is PureMac and Mole really free?

PureMac is open source under the MIT license, so the code is free to use. Mole is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, so the code is free to use. Our guide checked this against the official README.

What does PureMac and Mole run on?

PureMac: Mac. Mole: Mac (Windows version in progress).
