AI for Education, in plain words

Free AI tools for education help teachers and learners turn their own material into tutors, quizzes, diagrams and short videos. The best ones work from your notes and files, not from guesswork, and several run on your own computer. Every tool below is free and open source.

What free AI tools do for education

A tutor built from your own notes. DeepTutor answers questions, makes quizzes and builds study plans from files, notes or a YouTube link you add. It has study modes like Chat, Quiz, Solve, Research and Mastery Path. It doesn't include its own AI, so you connect a provider like OpenAI in Settings, per the DeepTutor README.

Chat with readings, with citations. Open Notebook is a free NotebookLM alternative you host yourself. Its answers come with citations, so you can check where each fact came from. It can also turn notes into podcasts with 1 to 4 speakers. With local AI models, the Open Notebook README says nothing leaves your machine.

A school library with no internet. Project NOMAD bundles Wikipedia, Khan Academy courses, medical guides, maps and a local AI chat on one offline server. It can help a classroom or a home with a weak connection. It runs on Linux, and the AI part needs a strong computer.

Lessons you can see. Archify turns a plain description into an interactive diagram that opens in any browser. Recordly records your screen with auto-zooms and a webcam bubble, good for short how-to lessons. OpenMontage lets an AI helper research, script, narrate and edit a whole video, with free voice and stock footage built in.

Tips for teachers and schools

Check the answers. Even a tutor that reads your notes can get something wrong, so treat it like a helper, not the final word. Ask your school before you add student work to any AI tool. Tools you host yourself keep files on your machine, but an online AI service sees what you send it. For tools picked for learners, see our free AI tools for students.

FAQ

Is there a free AI tutor?

DeepTutor is a free, open-source AI tutor. It answers questions, makes quizzes and builds study plans from your own notes, books and videos. You connect your own AI provider.

Is there a free NotebookLM alternative?

Open Notebook is free and open source and you host it yourself. It answers with citations and makes podcasts with 1 to 4 speakers.

Can I use AI for teaching with no internet?

Yes. Project NOMAD works offline after setup, with Wikipedia, Khan Academy courses and a local AI chat on one server.
