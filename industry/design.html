AI for Design, in plain words

Free AI tools for design now cover most of a designer's day: mockups, layered photo edits, diagrams and on-brand web pages. Many open the same files you already use, like Figma's .fig and Photoshop's PSD. Every tool below is free and open source.

What free AI tools do for design

Figma-style design with AI. OpenPencil is a free Figma alternative. It opens and saves .fig files. Press Cmd+J, describe what you want, and the AI builds it with 100+ design tools. You can share a link and co-edit live with no account. The makers say it's "usable today, with some rough edges," per the OpenPencil README.

Prototypes, decks and dashboards. OpenDesign drives an AI helper you already have, like Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. It ships with 151 design systems and reads your brand rules from a file called DESIGN.md. It's Apache 2.0 licensed, with a desktop app for Mac and Windows.

Pages that don't look AI-made. Hallmark from Together AI picks one of 21 themes for your brief, then tests the page against 57 generic-AI patterns. Awesome DESIGN.md gives you 84 ready-made style guides based on real sites. Drop one in your project and your AI coding tool builds pages in that style.

Photo editing like Photoshop. Compositor is a free Photoshop alternative for Apple silicon Macs on macOS 26 or later. It opens 8-bit RGB PSD files with folders, masks and blend modes still editable. PhotoCraft opens and saves layered PSD files on Mac, Windows and Linux, but its makers call it "early alpha," per the PhotoCraft GitHub page.

Diagrams in your brand colors. Diagram Design offers 45 diagram types, from flowcharts to timelines. Point it at your website and it pulls your colors and fonts. Each diagram is one HTML file you can export to PNG or SVG.

Which one to try first

If you live in Figma, start with OpenPencil. If you mostly edit photos on a Mac, try Compositor. If you design web pages with an AI helper, add Hallmark or a DESIGN.md file. Some of these tools are young, so keep a backup of any file you care about. For more ideas, see our free AI tools for creators.

FAQ

Is there a free Figma alternative with AI?

Yes. OpenPencil is free and open source, opens and saves .fig files, and builds designs from a chat prompt.

Can a free app open Photoshop PSD files?

Yes. Compositor opens 8-bit RGB PSD files on Apple silicon Macs, and PhotoCraft opens and saves layered PSD files on Mac, Windows and Linux. PhotoCraft is still an early alpha.

How do I stop AI web pages from looking generic?

Use Hallmark, which checks a page against 57 generic-AI patterns, or give your AI helper a DESIGN.md style guide from Awesome DESIGN.md.
