AI for Jobs & Careers, in plain words

Free AI tools for jobs and careers help you find the right roles, tailor your CV for each one and prepare for interviews. The good ones never apply for you and never invent experience you don't have. This page also tracks the AI news that changes who gets hired.

What free AI tools do for your job search

Know when to skip a job. Career-Ops checks each job against your CV and gives it a fit score. Below 4.0 out of 5, it advises you not to apply, though you can override it. It drafts tailored CVs and cover letters, but you always press Submit yourself. The Career-Ops README says it is "free for candidates, forever," with no tracking and no company server.

A tailored CV for every posting. AI Job Search turns one command and a job link into a tailored CV and cover letter as PDFs. A second "reviewer" AI researches the company and critiques the draft before you see it. Its author used it for 69 applications, got 20 first interviews and was hired as an AI engineer in June 2026, per the project's README. The code is free under MIT, but it runs in Claude Code, which needs a paid Claude plan or API credits.

Talk instead of type. Handy is a free offline voice typing app. Press a key, talk, and your words appear in any app, so you can draft cover letters and answers out loud. Your voice is turned into text on your own computer. It runs on Mac, Windows and Linux.

How AI is changing jobs

AI is reshaping work, and our monthly news pages track it with sources. In January 2026, Amazon cut 16,000 jobs (AI news January 2026). In April, Meta cut 8,000 jobs (AI news April 2026). The newest stories about hiring and job cuts show up in the news list on this page.

Use AI with care

Read every CV and letter before you send it. Make sure each claim matches your real work, because a recruiter will ask about it. Your CV goes to whichever AI service you connect, so pick one you trust.

FAQ

Is there a free AI tool to tailor my CV?

Yes. Career-Ops and AI Job Search both draft tailored CVs and cover letters for each job. Career-Ops is free, and AI Job Search is free code that needs a paid Claude plan to run.

Will these tools apply for jobs for me?

No. Career-Ops never sends an application or an email for you. You review the draft and press Submit yourself.

Does AI make up experience on my CV?

The AI Job Search README says all claims are checked against your real profile. Still, read every line before you send it.
