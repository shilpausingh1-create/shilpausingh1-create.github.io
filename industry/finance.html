AI for Finance, in plain words

Free AI tools for finance help you follow markets, keep AI costs down and make sense of the money moving through the AI world. This page pairs the tools we cover with our sourced news on AI deals, funding and stocks. Nothing here is investment advice.

What free AI tools do for finance

Markets and world news on one map. World Monitor puts news, markets, conflicts, weather events and flights on one live globe, with short AI-written news briefs. It comes in six flavors, including finance, commodity and energy versions. The core dashboard is free, and a paid Pro plan adds an AI analyst and daily digests, per the World Monitor GitHub page.

Cut your AI bill. Headroom shrinks logs, files and data on your computer before your AI reads them. In the README's tests, token savings ranged from 21% to 57% depending on the job. A token is a small chunk of text that AI services charge by. The Headroom README says no prompt or file content is sent anywhere to be squeezed. It helps most on long sessions, not short chats.

Spending limits for AI agents. Paperclip runs a team of AI agents on your own computer. You can set budgets, get alerts and pause work automatically, so spending stays inside the limits you pick.
