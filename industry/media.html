AI for Media, in plain words

Free AI tools for media help newsrooms, podcasters and video teams transcribe, edit and follow the news faster. Several run on your own computer, so raw tapes and footage stay with you. Every tool below is free to start, and most are fully open source.

What free AI tools do for media

Transcribe a full hour at once. Microsoft's VibeVoice turns up to 60 minutes of audio into text and labels who spoke and when. It works in over 50 languages, and you don't need to tell it which one. It's MIT licensed, but Microsoft says not to use it in business products without more testing, per the VibeVoice GitHub page.

Watch the world on one screen. World Monitor puts news, markets, conflicts, weather events and flights on one live map, with short AI-written news briefs. The core dashboard is free, and a paid Pro plan adds an AI analyst and daily digests. The code is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Edit video in your browser. OpenCut is a free CapCut alternative you can use at opencut.app with nothing to install. OpenReel is a browser video editor with multiple tracks, captions, audio mixing and 4K export, and no export watermark. The OpenReel README says core editing happens on your computer.

Clean green screen shots. CorridorKey pulls people off green or blue screens and keeps fine hair and motion blur. It exports 16-bit and 32-bit EXR files for Nuke, Fusion, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere. Its license lets you use it on paid client work, but you can't resell the tool.

Check before you publish

AI transcripts and news briefs can mishear names or miss context, so check every quote against the tape and every fact against its source before it goes out. Read each tool's license too: CorridorKey's is non-commercial share-alike, and VibeVoice is a research tool for now.

FAQ

What is the best free AI transcription tool for journalists?

VibeVoice from Microsoft is free and handles up to 60 minutes of audio at once, with speaker labels and timestamps. Microsoft calls it a research tool, so check transcripts before you quote them.

Is there a free video editor with no watermark?

Yes. OpenReel exports up to 4K with no watermark, and OpenCut runs free in your browser at opencut.app.

Can I use CorridorKey on paid work?

Yes. Its license allows paid client work, but you can't resell the tool or offer it as a paid API.
