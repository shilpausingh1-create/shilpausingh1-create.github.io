AI for Healthcare, in plain words

Free AI tools for healthcare won't replace a doctor, but they can help with the notes, the learning and the tracking around care. The ones we cover run on your own computer or phone, so private details can stay with you instead of on a company's server.

What free AI tools do for healthcare

Meeting and visit notes. Meetily is a free AI note taker. It writes live transcripts and AI summaries, and the Meetily README says transcripts and recordings are stored on your computer. That matters when a meeting touches patient or staff details. It runs on Mac, Windows and Linux. The free Community Edition is MIT licensed, and a paid PRO version adds extra features.

Medical guides with no internet. Project NOMAD is a free offline knowledge server. It bundles Wikipedia, medical guides, Khan Academy courses, maps and a local AI chat. After setup it works with no internet, which can help a clinic or a field team with a weak connection. The project's README recommends Ubuntu Linux, and the AI part needs a strong computer.

Fitness and workout tracking. openGym is a free workout tracker you own. It has over 5,600 exercises with animated demos, and you can search them by muscle on a body map. It runs on your own computer, your own server or fully on an Android phone. Per its GitHub page, it can bring in your history from FitNotes, Strong and Hevy, plus Apple Health weight exports.

What to check before you use them

AI notes and summaries can get things wrong, so a person should read them before they go into any record. Check your workplace's privacy rules before you record anyone, and always ask first. These tools don't give medical advice. Use them for notes, learning and tracking, and talk to a doctor about your health.

FAQ

Is there a free AI note taker for doctors?

Meetily is a free, open-source AI note taker that writes live transcripts and summaries and stores recordings on your own computer. Follow your clinic's privacy rules and check every note before you use it.

Can AI health tools work offline?

Yes. Project NOMAD works with no internet after setup, and it includes medical guides and a local AI chat. The AI part needs a strong computer.

Is my health data private with these tools?

Meetily, openGym and Project NOMAD keep your data on your own device instead of a company server, per their READMEs. If you connect an online AI service, what you send goes to that service.
