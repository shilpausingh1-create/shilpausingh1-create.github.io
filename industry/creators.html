AI for Creators, in plain words

Free AI tools for creators now cover the whole job: recording, editing, green screen, captions and even writing the script. Many run in your browser or on your own computer, with no watermark and no monthly fee. Every tool below is free to use, and most are open source.

What free AI tools do for creators

Edit video for free. OpenCut is a free CapCut alternative you can use at opencut.app with nothing to install. OpenReel is a browser editor with multiple tracks, captions, audio mixing, color and 4K export. The OpenReel README says there's no export watermark and "your finished video stays yours."

Let AI make the whole video. OpenMontage lets your AI helper research, script, narrate, edit and render a video. Free voice, stock footage, captions and editing come built in, and paid AI extras are optional, with a default spending cap of $10. Brag turns your project into a short launch video with one command, plus copy for your posts.

Record your screen like a pro. Recordly records and edits in one app, with auto-zooms, a smooth cursor and a webcam bubble. It runs on Mac, Windows and Linux and exports MP4 or GIF.

Clean green screen. CorridorKey pulls people off green or blue screens and keeps fine hair and motion blur. The README says recent builds run with 6 to 8 GB of graphics memory and on most M1 or newer Macs. You can use it on paid client work, but you can't resell it.

Photo edits and thumbnails. PhotoCraft opens and saves layered Photoshop PSD files on Mac, Windows and Linux, though it's still early alpha. Mac users can try Compositor, a free Photoshop alternative with layers, masks and blend modes.

Which one to try first

If you post short videos, start with OpenCut in your browser. If you teach or demo on screen, try Recordly. If you want AI to do the heavy lifting, try OpenMontage. Check each license before you use a tool on paid work. For design tools, see our free AI tools for design, and for growth tools see AI for marketing.

FAQ

Is there a free video editor with no watermark?

Yes. OpenReel exports up to 4K with no watermark, and OpenCut is a free CapCut alternative that runs in your browser.

Can AI make a whole video for me for free?

OpenMontage can research, script, narrate and edit a video with free voice and stock footage built in. Paid AI image or video services are optional.

What is a free alternative to Photoshop?

PhotoCraft opens and saves PSD files on Mac, Windows and Linux, and Compositor is a free Photoshop-style editor for Apple silicon Macs.
