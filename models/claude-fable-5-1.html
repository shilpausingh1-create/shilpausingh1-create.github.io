FAQ

Is Claude Fable 5.1 free and open?

No. Claude Fable 5.1 is a closed model: you can only use it through Anthropic's own app or paid API.

How much does Claude Fable 5.1 cost?

The listed price is $10 for every million tokens you send, $50 for every million tokens it writes, checked 17 Oct 2026. A token is a small piece of a word.

What is Claude Fable 5.1 best at?

Strong all-rounder for writing and coding, but one of the priciest. Its best result: #1 of 15 models scored on LiveBench 2.0 Coding (coding).

Who makes Claude Fable 5.1?

Claude Fable 5.1 is made by Anthropic. It came out on 1 Sep 2026.
