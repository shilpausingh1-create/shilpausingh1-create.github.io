FAQ

Is GPT-6 Luna free and open?

No. GPT-6 Luna is a closed model: you can only use it through OpenAI's own app or paid API.

How much does GPT-6 Luna cost?

The listed price is $0.1 for every million tokens you send, $0.5 for every million tokens it writes, checked 17 Oct 2026. A token is a small piece of a word.

What is GPT-6 Luna best at?

Very cheap and quick for simple everyday jobs. Its best result: #2 of 4 models scored on OpenRouter usage (this week) (most used (this week)).
