FAQ

Is GLM-5.3 free and open?

GLM-5.3 has open weights: you can download it and run it on your own computer or server. License: GLM-5.3 license: free to use with conditions.

How much does GLM-5.3 cost?

There is no listed price per token. It has open weights, so you can run it yourself or through a host you pick.

What is GLM-5.3 best at?

Strong open all-rounder with one of the best open coding scores on LiveBench. Its best result: #4 of 6 models scored on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (agent tasks).

Who makes GLM-5.3?

GLM-5.3 is made by Z.ai. It came out on 25 Aug 2026.
