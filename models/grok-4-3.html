FAQ

Is Grok 4.3 free and open?

No. Grok 4.3 is a closed model: you can only use it through xAI's own app or paid API.

How much does Grok 4.3 cost?

The listed price is $1.25 for every million tokens you send, $2.5 for every million tokens it writes, checked 17 Oct 2026. A token is a small piece of a word.

What is Grok 4.3 best at?

An older, cheaper xAI model; it scores below the newer ones. Its best result: #13 of 15 models scored on LMArena Text (chat & writing).

Who makes Grok 4.3?

Grok 4.3 is made by xAI.
